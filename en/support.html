Frequently Asked Questions


How do I restore my subscription?
Simply click "Restore Purchases" in the app settings section.


Where is my data stored?
Journal data is stored on your device. The developer cannot access it. It may be part of an iCloud backup only if you enable iPhone backup to iCloud in your Apple settings.
